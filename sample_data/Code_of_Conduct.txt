CODE OF CONDUCT AND WORKPLACE BEHAVIOR POLICY
Company: TechCorp Inc.
Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Version: 3.0

================================================================================
1. POLICY OVERVIEW

1.1 Purpose
This Code of Conduct establishes expectations for professional behavior,
ethical conduct, and workplace safety for all TechCorp Inc. employees,
contractors, and visitors.

1.2 Scope
This policy applies to all employees, regardless of employment status
(full-time, part-time, temporary), and during all work activities including:
- On company premises
- Off-site work locations
- Company-sponsored events
- While representing the company

1.3 Core Values
TechCorp is committed to:
- Respect and inclusion
- Integrity and honesty
- Professional excellence
- Accountability and responsibility
- Teamwork and collaboration

================================================================================
2. GENERAL CONDUCT EXPECTATIONS

2.1 Professional Behavior
Employees are expected to:
- Conduct themselves professionally and courteously
- Treat all colleagues with dignity and respect
- Follow all company policies and procedures
- Perform job duties with excellence
- Represent company positively to clients and public

2.2 Workplace Atmosphere
- Maintain professional workplace free from disruption
- Avoid excessive personal conversations during work time
- Participate respectfully in team meetings
- Support collaborative team environment
- Respect others' work time and focus

2.3 Attendance and Punctuality
- Arrive on time for all scheduled work shifts
- Report any absences to manager immediately
- Follow absence notification procedures (see Leave Policy)
- Excessive tardiness subject to disciplinary action
- Expected to work scheduled hours or approved modified schedule

2.4 Drug and Alcohol Policy
TechCorp is committed to maintaining a drug-free workplace:
- No alcohol during business hours or company events (exception: company
  sponsored social events with alcohol service permitted for 21+)
- No use, possession, or distribution of illegal drugs on company property
- No appearing at work impaired by drugs or alcohol
- Prescription medications: Notify HR if affects job performance
- Violation may result in disciplinary action or termination

================================================================================
3. HARASSMENT AND DISCRIMINATION

3.1 Anti-Harassment Policy
TechCorp maintains zero-tolerance for harassment based on:
- Race, color, or national origin
- Gender or gender identity
- Sexual orientation
- Religion
- Disability
- Age
- Marital or family status
- Military status
- Any other protected characteristic

3.2 Definition of Harassment
Harassment includes:
- Unwelcome verbal comments (jokes, slurs, insults)
- Unwelcome physical contact
- Unwelcome sexual advances or requests for sexual favors
- Display of offensive materials
- Intimidation or threats
- Retaliation for reporting harassment

3.3 Sexual Harassment
Particularly serious form of harassment:
- Unwanted sexual advances or propositions
- Sexual comments, jokes, or images
- Unwelcome touching or physical intimacy
- Conditioning employment on sexual favors
- Creating hostile work environment through sexual conduct

3.4 Reporting Harassment
If you experience or witness harassment:
1. Clearly tell the person their conduct is unwelcome (if safe to do so)
2. Document the incident (date, time, what happened, witnesses)
3. Report to your manager or HR immediately
4. Provide written report to HR within 3 days
5. Company will investigate within 5 business days

3.5 Investigation Process
- HR will conduct confidential investigation
- Interviews with complainant, respondent, witnesses
- Both parties notified of findings within 10 business days
- Appropriate corrective action taken if violation confirmed
- No retaliation against complainants or witnesses

3.6 Retaliation Prohibited
- No adverse employment action against person filing complaint
- No adverse employment action against witnesses
- Retaliation is violation of policy and may be illegal
- Report any retaliation to HR immediately

================================================================================
4. DISCRIMINATION POLICY

4.1 Anti-Discrimination Commitment
TechCorp does not discriminate in hiring, promotion, compensation, training,
or any employment decisions based on protected characteristics.

4.2 Equal Opportunity
- All employment decisions based on qualifications and merit
- Reasonable accommodations provided for disability
- Pregnancy accommodations provided as needed
- Religious observance accommodations considered
- Equal pay for equal work

4.3 Complaint Process
Employees experiencing discrimination may:
1. Report to manager or HR
2. File written complaint with HR
3. Request investigation within 5 business days
4. Participate in investigation (interviews, documentation)
5. Receive decision within 10 business days

4.4 Non-Retaliation
No retaliation against employees for reporting discrimination or cooperating
with investigations.

================================================================================
5. WORKPLACE VIOLENCE PREVENTION

5.1 Violence Prevention Commitment
TechCorp is committed to safe workplace free from threats or violence:
- No violence or threats of violence on company property
- No weapons or weapons of any kind permitted (see specific exceptions below)
- No intimidating or threatening behavior
- All threats taken seriously and investigated

5.2 Prohibited Weapons
Following items strictly prohibited on company property or during company business:
- Firearms (guns, rifles, handguns)
- Explosives
- Knives (except food service knives, small pocket knives <2 inches)
- Weapons of any kind

Exception: Licensed security personnel may carry firearms as required by
contract; must notify HR of licensing status.

5.3 Threat Assessment
Threats of violence will be:
- Taken seriously regardless of whether serious or joking
- Investigated immediately
- Subject to disciplinary action
- May be reported to law enforcement

5.4 Workplace Violence Response
If threatening behavior occurs:
1. Remove yourself from situation if possible
2. Alert manager or HR immediately
3. Company may involve law enforcement
4. Investigation conducted
5. Appropriate disciplinary action taken

5.5 Support Resources
If experiencing thoughts of violence:
- Contact Employee Assistance Program (EAP) confidentially
- Speak with HR about support resources
- Mental health support available at no cost
- No discrimination for seeking help

6. RESPECTFUL WORKPLACE INITIATIVE

6.1 Respect and Inclusion
- All employees treated with dignity and respect
- Diverse perspectives valued and encouraged
- Inclusive culture where all feel safe and valued
- Zero tolerance for bullying or unwelcome behavior

6.2 Bullying Prohibited
Bullying includes:
- Repeated targeting of same individual
- Intentional intimidation or degradation
- Deliberate exclusion from team activities
- Spreading false rumors or gossip
- Public humiliation or belittling

6.3 Conflict Resolution
Minor conflicts resolved through:
- Direct conversation with person (if safe)
- Manager mediation
- HR conflict resolution process

6.4 Grievance Process
Formal grievance procedure for significant concerns:
1. Submit written grievance to HR within 10 days of incident
2. HR meets with grievant and respondent separately
3. Investigation conducted (5 business days)
4. Meeting with both parties regarding findings
5. Written decision provided within 10 business days
6. Appeal available within 5 days to VP HR

7. DRESS CODE AND APPEARANCE

7.1 Dress Code Standards
Professional appearance expected:
- Business casual for most roles
  (Dress pants/skirts, collared shirts, closed-toe shoes)
- Business formal for client-facing roles and presentations
  (Suits, business dresses, professional shoes)
- Tech appropriate for engineering/development teams
  (Clean jeans, company t-shirts, comfortable shoes)

7.2 Specific Standards
- Clothing must be clean and in good condition
- No offensive language or images on clothing
- No excessively revealing clothing
- Neat, groomed appearance expected
- Footwear must be professional and safe

7.3 Exceptions
- Remote workers: Casual/comfortable attire acceptable
- Company casual days (usually Friday): Jeans and casual shirts acceptable
- Company-sponsored casual events: Specified attire for event
- Uniforms required for some roles: Provided by company

7.4 Grooming
- Personal grooming expected (hair, nails, hygiene)
- Fragrance acceptable if subtle (be mindful of allergies)
- Tattoos/piercings: Professional presentation expected
- Hair color: Any color acceptable if professional appearance maintained

8. SOCIAL MEDIA AND COMPANY REPRESENTATION

8.1 Social Media Conduct
- Personal social media accounts: Exercise good judgment and discretion
- Do not post confidential company information
- Do not represent company without authorization
- Do not post defamatory or offensive material about company
- Remember: Social media posts can be seen by colleagues and clients

8.2 Company Social Media
- Official company social media managed by marketing/communications
- Only authorized personnel post on company accounts
- Posts must be professional and on-brand
- Company social media monitored and maintained

8.3 Online Representation
- When identifying as TechCorp employee online: Be professional
- Do not share confidential information
- Do not make false statements about company
- Personal opinions should not reflect company position

8.4 Company Images and Logos
- Do not use company logos or images without permission
- Promotional materials must be approved by marketing
- Protect company intellectual property in online spaces

================================================================================
9. CONFIDENTIALITY AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

9.1 Confidential Information
During employment and after termination:
- Do not disclose company confidential information
- Confidential includes: Business plans, financial data, client lists, trade
  secrets, proprietary technology, product development, strategic decisions
- Confidentiality extends to: Coworker salary information, personnel matters,
  medical information, social security numbers

9.2 Intellectual Property
- Work created during employment belongs to company
- This includes: Code, documents, designs, inventions, processes
- Patents and copyrights assigned to company
- Side projects: Must not compete with company business

9.3 Communication Monitoring
- Company may monitor work email, Slack, files
- No expectation of privacy in company systems
- Personal use should be minimal and professional
- Violation of confidentiality may result in legal action

10. CONFLICTS OF INTEREST

10.1 Definition
Conflict of interest exists when:
- Personal interest interferes with company interest
- Financial interest in company competitor
- Romantic relationship with direct report or manager
- Family relationship with coworker
- Moonlighting for competitor or client

10.2 Disclosure Requirements
Employees must disclose:
- Financial interests in vendors or clients
- Family/romantic relationships with coworkers
- Planned moonlighting employment
- Potential conflicts at time of hire or when they arise

10.3 Management of Conflicts
- Manager removal from hiring/review decisions for related employee
- Recusal from decisions affecting vendors or clients
- Disclosure of conflicts to HR
- Prior approval for outside employment

10.4 Outside Employment
- Limited outside employment permitted (see separate Outside Employment Policy)
- Cannot work for competitor
- Cannot work for client without approval
- Cannot allow outside work to interfere with company employment

11. ADHERENCE TO LAWS AND REGULATIONS

11.1 Legal Compliance
- All employees required to comply with applicable laws and regulations
- Includes: Employment law, health and safety, environmental, data privacy
- Company ensures compliance and provides training
- Employees responsible for understanding relevant regulations

11.2 Reporting Violations
- If aware of law violation: Report to manager or HR
- External reporting: Government agencies, law enforcement
- Whistleblower protection: No retaliation for good-faith reporting
- Anonymous reporting: Hotline available (see Ethical Concerns section)

12. PERFORMANCE AND CONDUCT STANDARDS

12.1 Performance Expectations
- Perform job duties to best of ability
- Meet established performance standards
- Complete work on time and to quality standards
- Participate in training and development

12.2 Insubordination
- Comply with reasonable work direction from manager
- Respectfully challenge decisions if concerns exist
- If directed to do something unethical/illegal: Refuse and report
- Disciplinary action for refusal to perform reasonable work duties

12.3 Absenteeism and Tardiness
- Expected to report to work as scheduled
- Unexcused absences subject to disciplinary action
- Pattern of tardiness grounds for termination
- See Leave Policy for approved absences

13. DISCIPLINARY ACTION

13.1 Disciplinary Process
Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in:
1. Verbal warning (documented)
2. Written warning
3. Suspension (paid or unpaid)
4. Demotion
5. Termination

Severity and history determine discipline level.

13.2 Serious Violations
Immediate suspension or termination possible for:
- Violence or threats of violence
- Harassment or discrimination
- Theft or dishonesty
- Working under influence of drugs/alcohol
- Violation of confidentiality
- Breach of security policy
- Insubordination or defiance

13.3 Appeal Process
Employees may appeal disciplinary action:
- Submit written appeal to VP HR within 5 business days
- Meet to discuss concerns
- VP HR reviews and makes final decision
- Decision communicated within 10 business days

14. AT-WILL EMPLOYMENT

14.1 Employment at Will
This employment relationship is at-will:
- Either party (employee or company) can terminate employment
- Termination can occur at any time, for any legal reason
- Exception: Covered by employment agreement or union contract
- This code does not create employment contract

14.2 Modifications
- Company retains right to modify policies
- Employees notified of changes
- Continued employment constitutes acceptance of changes
