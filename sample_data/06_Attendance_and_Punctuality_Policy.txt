ATTENDANCE AND PUNCTUALITY POLICY

1. OVERVIEW
This policy establishes expectations regarding employee attendance and punctuality. Regular and reliable attendance is essential to the successful operation of our organization.

2. GENERAL EXPECTATIONS
- Employees are expected to arrive on time for their scheduled shifts
- Employees should notify their manager as soon as possible if unable to report to work
- Unauthorized absences may result in disciplinary action
- Excessive tardiness is grounds for disciplinary action

3. REPORTING ABSENCES
- Absences must be reported to your manager at least 30 minutes before your shift begins (or as soon as possible)
- Use the company's time-tracking system to log absences
- Provide a reason for the absence when reporting
- Include expected return date if absence is more than one day

4. UNEXCUSED ABSENCES
- Unexcused absences are absences not reported to management prior to the scheduled work time
- Three unexcused absences in 90 days may result in disciplinary action up to termination
- Employees who are absent for three consecutive workdays without notification may be considered to have abandoned their employment

5. TARDINESS
- Tardiness is defined as arrival after the scheduled start time
- Employees are responsible for arriving on time
- Chronic tardiness (more than 3 instances in 30 days) may result in disciplinary action
- Accumulated tardiness may affect performance evaluations and wage increases

6. ACCEPTABLE REASONS FOR ABSENCE
- Personal or family illness
- Medical or dental appointments
- Emergency situations
- Pre-approved time off
- PTO or vacation days
- Bereavement leave
- FMLA-qualifying leave

7. CONSEQUENCES
- First violation: Verbal warning
- Second violation: Written warning
- Third violation: Suspension or termination
- Management reserves the right to take more severe action for egregious violations

8. REFERENCES
- Leave and Time Off Policy
- Disciplinary Policy
- FMLA Compliance Guidelines
