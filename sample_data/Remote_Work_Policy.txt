REMOTE WORK POLICY
Company: TechCorp Inc.
Effective Date: March 1, 2024
Version: 1.5

================================================================================
1. POLICY OVERVIEW

1.1 Purpose
This policy establishes guidelines for remote work at TechCorp Inc., enabling
employees to work from locations other than company office facilities while
maintaining productivity, security, and company culture.

1.2 Policy Applicability
This policy applies to all full-time employees and approved part-time employees.
Temporary/contract workers require special approval from HR.

1.3 Policy Philosophy
Remote work is a privilege based on role requirements, performance history, and
business needs. Remote work arrangements require mutual agreement between the
employee and manager.

================================================================================
2. REMOTE WORK ELIGIBILITY

2.1 Role-Based Eligibility
The following roles are eligible for remote work:
- Software Engineering (100% remote eligible)
- Product Management (hybrid eligible)
- Design (hybrid eligible)
- Data Analytics (hybrid eligible)
- Finance (hybrid eligible)
- HR (hybrid eligible)
- Marketing (hybrid eligible)

The following roles are NOT eligible for remote work:
- Facilities Management (on-site required)
- Reception/Administrative (on-site required)
- Sales (on-site or client-site required)
- Operations (on-site required)

2.2 Performance-Based Eligibility
Employees must meet the following criteria:
- Employed for minimum 6 months
- Performance rating of "meets expectations" or higher
- No active disciplinary action
- Demonstrated ability to work independently
- Reliable communication skills

2.3 Exceptions
Ineligible employees may request exception consideration through their manager
and VP. Exceptions are considered case-by-case based on business needs.

================================================================================
3. REMOTE WORK ARRANGEMENTS

3.1 Remote Work Frequency Options

Full-Remote: Work entirely from home (software engineers only)
- 5 days per week from remote location
- Quarterly in-person office visits required (at least 4 days per quarter)
- Annual company meeting attendance mandatory

Hybrid Schedule (2 days remote): Most common arrangement
- Monday-Friday, choose 2 days per week to work remotely
- 3 days per week in office for collaboration
- Can adjust remote days weekly with manager approval
- Must maintain consistent schedule for team coordination

Hybrid Schedule (3 days remote):
- 3 days per week remote (higher discretion)
- 2 days per week in office
- Requires VP approval
- Must maintain in-office presence on key meeting days

Flexible Remote:
- Case-by-case basis
- Requires executive approval
- Must maintain minimum office presence (typically 1 day/week)

3.2 Remote Work Agreement
All remote work arrangements require:
1. Written agreement between employee and manager
2. Manager approval
3. HR sign-off
4. Equipment and setup requirements completed
5. Security compliance confirmed

3.3 Duration and Review
Remote work arrangements are reviewed:
- After initial 90-day trial period
- Annually with performance review
- Can be modified or terminated with 30 days notice
- May be suspended for business reasons

================================================================================
4. IN-OFFICE REQUIREMENTS

4.1 Mandatory Office Days
All remote-eligible employees must be in office for:
- Team meetings (typically Tuesday and Wednesday)
- Company all-hands meetings (once per month, Friday)
- Quarterly planning sessions (full team, 2 days)
- Annual conference and training (3 days minimum)

4.2 Equipment Setup Days
New remote employees must spend first week in office:
- Day 1: Onboarding and equipment setup
- Days 2-5: Transition and training

4.3 Holiday Office Closure
During office closure periods (typically December 24-27, July 4 week),
remote work is not permitted and must use PTO or unpaid leave.

================================================================================
5. REMOTE WORK SETUP AND EQUIPMENT

5.1 Home Office Requirements
Remote workers must maintain a home office with:
- Dedicated, secure workspace (not public areas)
- Proper desk and ergonomic chair
- Reliable internet connection (minimum 50 Mbps)
- Quiet environment suitable for video calls
- Secure, lockable storage for company materials

5.2 Equipment Provided by TechCorp
Company-provided equipment (issued upon approval):
- Laptop or desktop computer
- Monitor(s) (up to 2)
- Keyboard and mouse
- Headset with microphone
- Docking station (optional)
- VPN equipment if required

5.3 Equipment Cost Reimbursement
One-time reimbursement for home office setup:
- Internet upgrade: Up to $100
- Desk/Chair: Up to $500 (pre-approval required)
- Monitor: Up to $300 per monitor (max 2)
- Lighting/Accessories: Up to $100

Reimbursement requires receipts and manager approval. Budget available only
once per calendar year.

5.4 Maintenance and Support
- IT support available 8am-6pm weekdays via remote support
- Hardware repairs: Ship to/from company facility
- Software support: Remote assistance available

5.5 Equipment Return
Upon termination or change to on-site role:
- All company equipment must be returned within 5 business days
- Employee responsible for shipping costs if applicable
- Final check must be completed with IT

================================================================================
6. WORK HOURS AND AVAILABILITY

6.1 Core Hours
All remote employees must be available during core business hours:
- Monday-Friday, 10am-3pm EST (synchronous collaboration time)
- Outside core hours, flexible based on role and manager approval
- After-hours availability based on role requirements (e.g., on-call)

6.2 Time Zone Considerations
- Remote employees in Pacific zone must adjust schedule to accommodate EST team
- Start times typically 7-9am PST / 10am-12pm EST
- Extreme time zones (9+ hours difference) require VP approval

6.3 Overtime and Vacation Coverage
- Remote work does not excuse overtime obligations
- During vacation coverage periods, must check in daily with manager
- OOO (out of office) messages required when unavailable

6.4 Response Time Expectations
- Email: Response within 2 hours during business hours
- Slack/chat: Immediate response during core hours
- Missed meetings: Provide explanation to manager within 24 hours

================================================================================
7. COMMUNICATION AND COLLABORATION

7.1 Communication Tools
Required tools for remote work:
- Slack: Real-time chat and team communication
- Zoom/Microsoft Teams: Video conferencing
- Google Workspace: Documents and collaboration
- GitHub/Project Management: Code and task management

All tools must be installed and tested before remote work begins.

7.2 Meeting Attendance
- All scheduled meetings are mandatory
- Must use video on for team meetings (camera-on culture)
- Must test audio/video 5 minutes before meeting
- Background must be professional or use virtual background

7.3 1-on-1 Meetings
- Weekly one-on-ones with manager (video required)
- Monthly skip-level meetings with VP or above (video required)
- Career development discussions quarterly (in-person preferred)

7.4 Asynchronous Communication
- Document decisions in writing (email/Slack)
- Do not rely on verbal communication only
- Update project status in tracking system daily
- Use @mentions to get attention for urgent items

================================================================================
8. SECURITY AND CONFIDENTIALITY

8.1 Data Security Requirements
Remote workers must comply with:
- Use company VPN for all company system access
- Enable all security features on company device (antivirus, firewall, etc.)
- No company data on personal devices without explicit approval
- Encrypt all home office wifi
- Use strong passwords (minimum 12 characters, changed quarterly)

8.2 Network Security
- Do not use public wifi (coffee shops, airports, etc.) for work
- Use mobile hotspot as backup option only
- Keep personal and work devices separate when possible
- Report any security concerns to IT within 24 hours

8.3 Physical Security
- Company materials must be stored securely
- Do not work in public areas (coffee shops, libraries, etc.)
- Secure all monitors when stepping away
- Lock home office when not in use
- No visitors or family access to work materials

8.4 Confidentiality
- No discussion of company business in shared spaces
- Keep confidential materials out of sight during video calls
- Use professional background (home office setting or virtual background)
- Do not discuss business on personal calls/devices

8.5 Security Compliance
- Mandatory security training (annual)
- Phishing simulation testing (quarterly)
- Device security checks (IT may verify at any time)
- Violation of security policies may result in termination of remote work

================================================================================
9. PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT

9.1 Performance Expectations
Remote workers are held to the same performance standards as office workers:
- Same deadlines and deliverables
- Same quality standards
- Same collaboration expectations
- Same attendance requirements (core hours)

9.2 Productivity Monitoring
- Output/deliverables are the primary measure of productivity
- Time tracking tools may be used (requires employee consent)
- Manager may request screenshots or work logs (justifiable cause only)
- Surveillance software is strictly prohibited

9.3 Performance Reviews
- Remote workers evaluated by same criteria as office workers
- Lack of visibility should not negatively impact review
- Manager responsible for regular feedback (weekly 1-on-1s)
- Promotion decisions made on merit regardless of work location

9.4 Underperformance
If performance issues arise:
1. Manager notifies employee within 48 hours
2. Written performance plan provided (30-60 days)
3. Weekly check-ins required
4. If performance does not improve: may require return to office
5. Continued underperformance may result in disciplinary action

================================================================================
10. EXPENSE AND LIABILITY

10.1 Utility Reimbursement
Remote workers may request partial utility reimbursement:
- Monthly reimbursement: Up to $50 (covers internet and electricity)
- Requires manager approval
- Paid via payroll as taxable reimbursement
- Maximum 12 months per calendar year

10.2 Workers' Compensation
- Home office injuries covered under workers' compensation if work-related
- Must report injuries to HR within 24 hours
- Only injuries during work hours covered
- Company not responsible for home maintenance/repairs

10.3 Liability and Insurance
- Company not responsible for home damage or theft
- Employee responsible for homeowner's/renter's insurance
- Company equipment covered under company insurance

================================================================================
11. TERMINATION OF REMOTE WORK

11.1 Reasons for Termination
Remote work may be discontinued due to:
- Employee request
- Business needs requiring office presence
- Performance issues
- Failure to meet security requirements
- Violation of remote work policy
- Change in job role/department

11.2 Termination Process
- 30 days written notice to employee (except discipline)
- Employee must return to office work schedule
- Transition period (1-2 weeks) may be required
- Equipment must be returned per policy

11.3 Appeals
Employees may appeal termination of remote work:
- Submit written appeal to VP within 5 business days
- Meet with HR to discuss concerns
- VP will make final decision within 10 business days

================================================================================
12. HYBRID TEAM CONSIDERATIONS

12.1 Inclusion and Culture
- Hybrid teams ensure remote and office staff have equal access to:
  - Meetings and decision-making
  - Career development opportunities
  - Social events and team bonding
  - Information and communication

12.2 Meeting Practices
- If any team member is remote, ALL participants dial in
- No in-office side conversations during remote calls
- Rotate in-office days to include all team members in office interactions

12.3 Team Building
- Monthly virtual happy hours for remote teams
- Quarterly in-person team building (everyone in office)
- Annual company retreat (all remote employees required to attend)

================================================================================
13. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: Can I work from a coffee shop?
A: No. Security policy requires dedicated, secure workspace. Coffee shops are
   not permitted.

Q: Can I take remote work on a business trip?
A: Only with VP approval. Company security and VPN requirements must be met.

Q: What if my internet goes down?
A: Use mobile hotspot as temporary solution. Notify manager of outage. Report
   repeated issues to IT.

Q: Can I work from another country?
A: Not without explicit approval from HR and Legal. Tax/visa implications must
   be addressed.

Q: What if I need to bring my child to work from home?
A: This is not encouraged. Remote work requires focus and professional
   environment. Childcare must be arranged.

Q: Can I change my remote work schedule?
A: Changes require manager approval. Core hours (10am-3pm EST) must be
   maintained.
