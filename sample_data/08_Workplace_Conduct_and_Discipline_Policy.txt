WORKPLACE CONDUCT AND DISCIPLINARY POLICY

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes a framework for maintaining a professional workplace and addressing performance or conduct issues in a fair and consistent manner.

2. EXPECTATIONS
All employees are expected to:
- Perform their duties professionally and competently
- Maintain a positive attitude and respectful demeanor
- Comply with all company policies and procedures
- Report concerns through appropriate channels
- Contribute to a safe and inclusive work environment

3. PROGRESSIVE DISCIPLINE
The company generally follows a progressive discipline approach:

LEVEL 1: VERBAL WARNING
- Documented conversation with manager
- Discussion of issue and expected behavior change
- Opportunity for employee to respond
- Record placed in personnel file

LEVEL 2: WRITTEN WARNING
- Formal written notice of violation
- Specific expectations for improvement
- Timeline for meeting expectations
- Copy provided to employee and personnel file

LEVEL 3: SUSPENSION
- Paid or unpaid suspension (determined by severity)
- Duration specified in writing
- Clear expectations for reinstatement
- Final opportunity to demonstrate compliance

LEVEL 4: TERMINATION
- Issued for severe violations or failure to improve
- Severance package provided per company policy
- Final paycheck includes accrued PTO
- Benefits continuation information provided

4. GROUNDS FOR IMMEDIATE TERMINATION
The following may result in immediate termination without progressive discipline:
- Violence or threats of violence
- Theft or dishonesty
- Gross insubordination
- Working under the influence of drugs or alcohol
- Sexual harassment or assault
- Violation of confidentiality
- Repeated serious policy violations

5. INVESTIGATION PROCESS
When a conduct issue is reported:
- HR initiates a prompt investigation
- All relevant parties are interviewed
- Documentation is reviewed
- Investigation is completed within 10 business days
- Findings are communicated to involved parties
- Confidentiality is maintained to the extent possible

6. APPEAL PROCESS
Employees may appeal disciplinary decisions:
- Appeal must be submitted in writing within 5 business days
- Appeal is reviewed by HR and a manager not involved in original decision
- Final determination will be communicated within 5 business days
- All documentation is placed in personnel file

7. DOCUMENTATION
All disciplinary actions are documented including:
- Date and nature of violation
- Witness statements or evidence
- Action taken and expectations communicated
- Employee acknowledgment of receipt

8. RETALIATION PROTECTION
Employees cannot be retaliated against for:
- Good faith reports of policy violations
- Participation in investigations
- Filing formal complaints
- Requesting accommodations or protected leave
