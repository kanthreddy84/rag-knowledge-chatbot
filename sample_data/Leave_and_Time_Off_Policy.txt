LEAVE AND TIME OFF POLICY
Company: TechCorp Inc.
Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Version: 2.0

================================================================================
1. ANNUAL PAID TIME OFF (PTO)

1.1 PTO Allocation
All full-time employees receive annual paid time off based on tenure:

- 0-2 years of service: 15 days per calendar year
- 3-5 years of service: 20 days per calendar year
- 6+ years of service: 25 days per calendar year

Part-time employees (20+ hours/week) receive PTO on a pro-rata basis.
Contractors and consultants are not eligible for PTO.

1.2 PTO Accrual
PTO is allocated on January 1st of each calendar year and does not accrue
monthly. Employees must use their PTO during the calendar year in which it is
allocated. Unused PTO does not carry over to the next year.

Exception: Up to 5 days of unused PTO may be carried over to the following
January if approved by the employee's manager.

1.3 PTO Request Process
1. Submit PTO request at least 2 weeks in advance through the HR portal
2. Request must be approved by direct manager
3. For periods longer than 5 consecutive days, VP approval required
4. PTO during peak business periods (Q4) requires Director approval
5. Emergency/last-minute PTO (less than 2 weeks): Manager discretion

1.4 PTO Usage Guidelines
- PTO should not exceed 5 consecutive days without executive approval
- During team projects, coordinate with team to ensure coverage
- Cannot use PTO during mandatory company training
- Approved PTO counts as worked time for benefits purposes

2. SICK LEAVE

2.1 Sick Leave Allocation
Full-time employees receive 10 days of sick leave per calendar year.
Part-time employees (20+ hours/week) receive 5 days per calendar year.

Sick leave is separate from PTO and does not roll over year to year.

2.2 Sick Leave Usage
Sick leave may be used for:
- Personal illness or medical appointments
- Care for family members (spouse, children, parents)
- Medical procedures or recovery
- Preventive care visits
- Bereavement leave (see Section 4)

2.3 Sick Leave Notification
- Notify manager as soon as possible (ideally before start of work day)
- Contact HR if absence exceeds 3 consecutive days
- Medical certification may be required for absences longer than 3 days
- Pattern of sick leave usage may be reviewed by HR

2.4 Pay During Sick Leave
Sick leave is paid at your regular rate of pay. Unused sick leave does not
carry over and is not paid out upon termination.

================================================================================
3. HOLIDAYS

3.1 Paid Company Holidays
The following days are paid holidays for all full-time employees:

- New Year's Day (January 1)
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day (Third Monday in January)
- Presidents' Day (Third Monday in February)
- Memorial Day (Last Monday in May)
- Juneteenth (June 19)
- Independence Day (July 4)
- Labor Day (First Monday in September)
- Veterans Day (November 11)
- Thanksgiving Day (Fourth Thursday in November)
- Day After Thanksgiving (Friday after Thanksgiving)
- Christmas Day (December 25)

Total: 11 paid holidays per year

3.2 Holiday Eligibility
- Full-time employees: Paid
- Part-time employees (20+ hours/week): Paid for holidays during scheduled work weeks
- Temporary/Contract employees: Not eligible (may be compensated at higher hourly rate)

3.3 Holiday Falls on Weekend
If a holiday falls on Saturday, it is observed on Friday.
If a holiday falls on Sunday, it is observed on Monday.

3.4 Working on Holidays
Employees required to work on a paid holiday will receive:
- Holiday pay (regular rate)
- Plus compensatory time equal to hours worked
- Or 1.5x pay (employee choice with manager approval)

================================================================================
4. BEREAVEMENT LEAVE

4.1 Bereavement Leave Eligibility
Upon the death of an immediate family member, employees are eligible for up to
5 days of paid bereavement leave.

Immediate family members include:
- Spouse or domestic partner
- Children
- Parents
- Siblings
- Grandparents
- Parents-in-law

4.2 Extended Family
For death of extended family members (aunts, uncles, cousins, in-laws),
employees may request up to 3 days of bereavement leave or personal days.

4.3 Bereavement Leave Usage
- Notify manager and HR as soon as possible
- Submit death certificate or obituary to HR
- Pay is continued during bereavement leave
- Additional time off may be available as unpaid leave or PTO

================================================================================
5. FAMILY AND MEDICAL LEAVE ACT (FMLA)

5.1 FMLA Eligibility
Employees who have worked at TechCorp for 12 months and have worked at least
1,250 hours in the past 12 months are eligible for FMLA protection.

5.2 FMLA Coverage
FMLA provides up to 12 weeks (480 hours) of unpaid leave in a 12-month period for:
- Birth of a child or placement of a child for adoption
- Care for spouse, child, or parent with serious health condition
- Employee's own serious health condition
- Military caregiver leave
- Qualifying exigencies related to military service

5.3 FMLA Process
1. Notify HR immediately upon need for FMLA
2. Complete FMLA certification form
3. Provide medical certification if required
4. HR will coordinate with benefits (health insurance continues)
5. Position held or equivalent position upon return

================================================================================
6. PARENTAL LEAVE

6.1 Parental Leave Policy
TechCorp offers supplemental parental leave to support work-life balance:

Primary Caregiver (birth parent or adoptive parent): 12 weeks paid leave
Secondary Caregiver (spouse/partner): 4 weeks paid leave

6.2 Eligibility
- Full-time employees only
- Must have completed 6 months of employment
- Birth, adoption, or foster placement of child

6.3 Parental Leave Process
1. Notify HR at least 30 days before expected leave date
2. Complete parental leave application
3. Coordinate with manager regarding coverage
4. Health benefits continue during leave
5. Position held upon return

6.4 Combination with FMLA
Parental leave counts toward FMLA protection and must be used within 12 months
of birth/placement.

================================================================================
7. UNPAID LEAVE

7.1 Personal Leave of Absence
Employees may request unpaid leave of absence for personal reasons.
- Duration: Up to 6 months (subject to approval)
- Benefits: May continue health insurance (employee pays premiums)
- Position: Position held or equivalent upon return (within 6 months)
- Process: Submit written request 30 days in advance

7.2 Sabbatical Leave
Employees with 5+ years of service may request sabbatical leave:
- Duration: Up to 3 months
- Frequency: Once every 7 years
- Pay: Unpaid (benefits continue)
- Process: Submit proposal to VP for review

================================================================================
8. TIME OFF POLICIES

8.1 Late Arrival/Early Departure
- Must notify manager at least 1 hour before schedule change
- Can use PTO or unpaid leave (with approval)
- Frequent tardiness may be subject to disciplinary action

8.2 Flexible Work Arrangements
Eligible employees may request flexible schedules:
- 4/10 schedule (4 days, 10 hours each)
- 9/80 schedule (alternating weeks of 9 and 8 hours)
- Compressed schedule (prior approval required)

8.3 Remote Work Days
Full-time employees may work up to 2 days per week remotely (subject to
manager approval and role requirements). Remote work policy in separate document.

================================================================================
9. HOLIDAYS AND OBSERVANCE

9.1 Religious Observance
Employees may take religious observance leave (up to 2 days per year) for
important religious holidays not listed as company holidays. Coordinate with
manager at least 2 weeks in advance.

9.2 Jury Duty
Employees summoned for jury duty will receive paid time off for:
- Time to serve as jury member
- Court-required appearances

Provide court documentation to HR.

================================================================================
10. GENERAL PROVISIONS

10.1 Pay During Leave
Except for unpaid leave, employees continue to receive their regular salary
during approved leave time.

10.2 Accrual During Leave
While on unpaid leave exceeding 1 month, PTO and sick leave accrue continues
(FMLA and parental leave exception).

10.3 Benefits Continuation
- Paid leave: All benefits continue
- Unpaid leave (less than 1 month): All benefits continue
- Unpaid leave (1-3 months): Employee may continue health insurance (employee pays premium)
- Unpaid leave (3+ months): Benefits may be discontinued; individual case review

10.4 Coordination with Other Benefits
Leave times coordinate with:
- Workers' compensation leave
- Disability leave
- Military service leave (USERRA)
- State-mandated leave requirements

10.5 Return to Work
Employees returning from leave must:
- Notify HR 1 week before return date
- Complete return-to-work paperwork
- Confirm benefits continuation
- Meet with manager regarding role/assignments

11. MANAGEMENT RESPONSIBILITIES

11.1 Manager Obligations
- Approve/deny PTO requests within 3 business days
- Ensure adequate team coverage
- Track employee leave usage
- Document approval decisions
- Report to HR any excessive leave usage

11.2 HR Responsibilities
- Maintain accurate leave records
- Monitor FMLA compliance
- Process leave requests
- Answer policy questions
- Provide documentation as needed
