DATA PRIVACY AND SECURITY POLICY

1. INTRODUCTION
Our organization is committed to protecting confidential information and respecting the privacy of employees, customers, and business partners. This policy establishes guidelines for data handling and security.

2. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
Confidential information includes:
- Trade secrets and proprietary business information
- Customer and employee personal data
- Financial and business records
- Strategic plans and forecasts
- Source code and technical documentation
- Unpublished product information

3. AUTHORIZED ACCESS
- Access to confidential information is limited to employees with job-related need-to-know
- Access is determined by managers and approved by department heads
- Access requests require documented justification
- Regular audits verify appropriate access levels

4. SAFEGUARDING REQUIREMENTS
- Store sensitive documents in secure, locked locations
- Use passwords and encryption for digital files
- Do not discuss confidential matters in public spaces
- Dispose of sensitive documents securely (shredding or secure waste services)
- Use company VPN when accessing systems from remote locations
- Lock computers when away from your desk
- Do not share login credentials with anyone

5. ACCEPTABLE USE
Company technology resources may be used for:
- Work-related communications and activities
- Limited personal use (email, browsing) provided it does not interfere with work
- Professional development related to your role

Unacceptable uses include:
- Accessing explicit or offensive content
- Downloading or distributing malware
- Unauthorized access to company or employee systems
- Using company resources for illegal purposes
- Violating intellectual property rights

6. DATA BREACH NOTIFICATION
- Any suspected data breach must be reported to IT immediately
- Affected parties will be notified within 72 hours of confirmed breach
- Documentation of breach will be maintained
- Remedial measures will be implemented to prevent recurrence

7. COMPLIANCE
- GDPR compliance for European personal data
- CCPA compliance for California residents
- HIPAA compliance for health information (if applicable)
- State data breach notification laws

8. VIOLATIONS
Violations of this policy may result in:
- Disciplinary action up to termination
- Civil or criminal liability
- Mandatory training or counseling
- Loss of system access
